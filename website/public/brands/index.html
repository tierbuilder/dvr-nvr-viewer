RTSP addresses for DVR, NVR and IP camera brands

DVR Viewer has these built in: pick the brand on its setup page and it fills in the address for you. The table is also handy for VLC or any other RTSP player: replace the IP address, USER and PASSWORD with yours. The addresses shown are for camera 1 at full quality.

Placeholders in the addresses

{channel} is the camera number (1, 2, 3…), {channel0} counts from 0 and {channel:02d} is two digits (01, 02…). For quality, {sub} is 0 for full and 1 for the lighter stream, {stream} is 1 or 2, and {quality} / {Quality} are main/sub or Main/Sub. Use them with Other on the setup page for brands not listed here.

Questions

What is an RTSP address?

It's the address a DVR, NVR or IP camera streams live video on, like rtsp://user:password@192.168.1.64:554/... DVR Viewer builds it for you from the brand, IP address, username and password.

My brand isn't listed. Will it work?

Most likely. Many unbranded DVRs are made by Dahua or XMEye manufacturers, so try those presets first. Otherwise choose Other and type the RTSP address from the manual, using {channel} where the camera number goes.

Which RTSP port should I use?

Usually 554. Foscam cameras normally use 88. The port is in the DVR's Network menu, often under RTSP.
